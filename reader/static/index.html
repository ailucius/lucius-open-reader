    What it does

    Give it a tender. It lists every requirement with the tender's own words and the page they are on, then tells you whether to bid.

    FindTavily searches the web for tender documents.
    ReadNemotron 3 Super reads six pages at a time and quotes each requirement.
    CheckEvery quote is matched to its page. Wrong pages are fixed; misses stay flagged.
    DecideNemotron 3 Ultra judges each mandatory requirement against your company. The call follows from those judgments.
